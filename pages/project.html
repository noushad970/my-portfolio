🏙️ Project 8: Capital Kingdom by Unity

Role: Game Developer

A top-down city tycoon simulator built in Unity for the Global Game Jam under the Zoud GameLab team. Players run a capital city one district at a time, balancing the city budget and their own health while a day/night clock advances and city occurrences break out across the map. The districts are filled with working locations — a municipal park, industrial manufacturing plants, oil and petroleum stations, a stadium and a school depot — each tied into the city economy, and the whole interface can be switched between English and Arabic.
